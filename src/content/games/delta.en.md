## How to play

Your basins fill with light. Drag from one of your basins to a neighbor: its light flows there, unit by unit, until you change its route. Tap a basin twice to hold its light instead. Light arriving at a rival or empty basin drains it, and takes it once it's empty. Currents meeting head-on in a channel cancel out, and some channels open and close with the tide. Take every rival basin to win.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set the route of a basin | `Space` or `Enter` on one of your basins, arrow keys to a neighbor, then `Space` or `Enter` | Drag from one of your basins to a neighbor, or click one then the other | Drag from one of your basins to a neighbor, or tap one then the other |
| Hold a basin's light | `Space` or `Enter` on one of your basins, then `Space` or `Enter` again | Click the basin twice | Tap the basin twice |
| Move the cursor | Arrow keys: nearest basin in that direction | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| Give up an order in progress | `Backspace` | Release away from a neighbor, or click elsewhere | Release away from a neighbor, or tap elsewhere |
| Restart the level | `R` | Restart button | Restart button |
| Pause | `Esc` or `P` | Pause button | Pause button |
