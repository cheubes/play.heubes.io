## How to play

Tilt the board to slide all the blocks at once. Blocks slide until they hit a wall or another block. Get every colored block onto a target of the same color and shape. Sticky tiles stop any block that slides onto them. Grey blocks have no target: use them as obstacles.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Tilt the board | Arrow keys | Swipe across the play area in the chosen direction |
| Undo the last move | `Z` or `Backspace` | Undo button |
| Restart the level | `R` | Restart button |
| Pause | `Esc` or `P` | Pause button |
