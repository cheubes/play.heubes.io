## How to play

Your little automaton runs on its own and turns around when it hits a wall. Tap anywhere to make it jump. Reach the exit of each room, and grab the cog hidden in each one if you dare. Springs bounce you high, thin platforms can be jumped through from below, levers open and close doors. Hit spikes or fall, and you're back at the start of the room.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch the automaton, then jump | `Space`, `Enter` or `↑` | Click anywhere in the play area | Tap anywhere in the play area |
| Restart the level | `R` | Restart button | Restart button |
| Pause | `Esc` or `P` | Pause button | Pause button |
