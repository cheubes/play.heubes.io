## How to play

Drag across the sheet to fold it: the fold line follows halfway between the edge you pull and where it lands. The folded part flips over and lands on top, so the last fold is the one you see. Match the pattern on the card, same size and same colors. Cut-out squares let the layers below show through, and pinned squares hold their part in place.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fold | `Shift` + arrow: folds the part opposite the arrow over the selected line, in the direction of the arrow | Drag across the sheet in the direction of the fold, then release | Drag your finger across the sheet in the direction of the fold, then release |
| Choose the fold line | Arrow keys: next line of the same orientation; an arrow of the other orientation moves to the middle line of that orientation | The line follows the drag | The line follows the drag |
| Give up a fold in progress | Not applicable | Go back to the starting point before releasing | Go back to the starting point before releasing |
| Undo the last fold | `Z` or `Backspace` | Undo button | Undo button |
| Restart the level | `R` | Restart button | Restart button |
| Pause | `Esc` or `P` | Pause button | Pause button |
